Copyclip — Installing and opening it for the first time
=======================================================

1. Drag Copyclip into the Applications folder.

2. Open Copyclip from Applications.
   Copyclip isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS stops it the first time with a
   message saying it can't verify Copyclip. Click "Done".

3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security
   section, and click "Open Anyway" next to the message about Copyclip.
   Confirm with "Open Anyway" and your password if macOS asks.

Copyclip then opens normally, and you won't be asked again.
It runs in the menu bar (look for the paperclip icon), not in the Dock.

On macOS 13 and 14 you can also Control-click Copyclip in Applications,
choose Open, and click Open in the dialog.

Advanced: instead of steps 2–3 you can run this in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Copyclip.app

Copyclip keeps your clipboard history on this Mac and never sends it anywhere.
